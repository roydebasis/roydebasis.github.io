01 — About

A bit about me

I specialize in the part of software most people never see: the APIs, databases, and business logic that decide whether an application actually works when real money and real customers are involved.

I'm a software engineer with 8.5 years of experience building business-critical web applications in PHP, Laravel, and Node.js. My work spans the full backend spectrum — RESTful APIs, well-designed MySQL schemas, and connecting external platforms like Gmail, GoHighLevel, DocuSeal, Twilio, and payment gateways. Most recently, I've been building the backend of a funding and loan management platform, turning stakeholder requirements into automated intake, AI-driven bank-statement analysis, underwriting scorecards, and e-signature workflows.

Recently, I've focused heavily on AI-powered application development and workflow automation — using the OpenAI API and n8n to process documents, extract financial data, and drive underwriting scorecards. I care deeply about clean code, maintainability, and delivering real value.
